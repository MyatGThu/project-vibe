# Knights — Go-to-Market Playbook (Marketing + Delivery)

*Researched for a 5-person team, one hero SKU (men's leather jacket), premium/editorial
positioning, Shopify, small budget, international. 2025–2026 sources; figures are directional —
re-validate the few that drive your PO and pricing before committing capital (some sources were
reachable only via search summaries, not full-page fetch).*

---

## The strategic reality
Launching **one high-cost, high-AOV, high-consideration product** where **fit-driven returns are
the biggest margin killer** and, post-2025 tariff / de-minimis changes, cross-border landed cost is
materially higher. So the playbook is **not** "spray paid ads and scale." It is: **build demand
before you build inventory, price for a premium net-of-returns margin, and run a lean
owned-channel + creator engine.**

---

# Part 1 — Marketing

## Positioning
- Anchor the brand to the one jacket deliberately — "the last leather jacket you'll buy." Sell
  **meaning, not spec** (ethos + community). Four pillars: a specific point of view, provenance &
  craft, restraint as aesthetic (the Swiss-editorial feel *is* a product), founder as protagonist.
- Produce brand anchors before launch: one **60–90s brand film** + a repeatable creative system
  shipping fresh assets every 2–3 weeks.
- Expand the line **off proven demand** (same silhouette → new leathers → adjacent outerwear).

## Pricing (the decision that makes the margin math work)
- Not plain keystone. **Triple-keystone (~4× cost, ~75% gross) is the floor** once CAC > ~$30;
  apparel DTC runs 3–5× cost / 60–75% gross.
- **Price off net-of-returns margin, not gross** (apparel returns 25–40%). Points to a retail
  price in the **$250–$600+** band — a too-low price also undermines "elevated."

## Channel mix (ROI-per-hour for a lean team; owned + earned first)
- **Organic short-form (Reels/TikTok)** — top of the flywheel. Craft/process, styling ("one
  jacket, 5 ways"), founder POV, try-on/unboxing, trend participation in your voice. Find winning
  hooks organically *before* paying.
- **Paid social** — Meta primary, TikTok secondary; concentrate at launch. **Creative is the
  targeting lever** (15–20% budget on creative testing). UGC-style for prospecting (~27% higher
  CTR), studio for retargeting. **Retargeting = highest ROAS** for a $300+ considered buy.
- **Micro-creator seeding** — highest-leverage cheap channel. Micro (10–50k) = sweet spot
  (35–50% post rate on gifting); volume of micro beats a few macro (25 micro drove 4.5× the
  revenue of 2 macro at equal budget). Gate tightly (~15–30 aligned creators) since unit cost is high.
- **Email + SMS (Klaviyo)** — the profitability engine (email = 20–30% of DTC revenue; flows =
  ~41% of email revenue). Build 3 flows first: **welcome/waitlist nurture**, **checkout-started
  (3 emails + 1 SMS)**, **post-purchase (confirm → in-production → care guide → review request)**.
- **SEO/content** — slow-burn; seed buying-intent + care queries. **Community** — the premium
  retention moat (waitlist → "first 100" → VIP drops).

## Launch tactics
- **Pre-launch waitlist = the single most important tactic** for cash-constrained, high-cost
  inventory. De-risks inventory and converts far better than a cold list (top waitlisters convert
  70–80% in the first ~20 min of a drop). Start collecting emails months ahead.
- **Limited, numbered first drop / pre-order** (doubles as cash-flow + inventory strategy); 61% of
  fashion buyers happily wait 2–4 weeks. Transition to always-on once demand is proven.
- **UGC + reviews from day one** — UGC on PDPs lifts apparel conversion ~207%; put a reviews app
  live before launch (an empty review section on a $400 jacket kills conversion).

## If you can only do 3 things
1. **Pre-launch waitlist + owned email (Klaviyo).** 2. **Organic short-form + micro-creator
seeding.** 3. **Retargeting-weighted paid — only after #1/#2 produce winning creative + reviews.**
Rough starting media budget: **~$1,500–$4,500/month**, ramped against proven unit economics.

## Metrics to gate spend on
CAC (~$68–90 ok on a $200+ AOV, 60%+ margin) · AOV ($200–$1,000+) · Gross margin 60–75% ·
**Contribution margin** (median 15–20%, the solvency number) · Repeat rate (low for a durable
jacket — plan line extensions) · Return rate (~25%, fit = 50%) · Email % of revenue (aim 25%+).
A 5-pt repeat-rate lift usually out-earns a 10% AOV increase.

# Part 2 — Delivery / Fulfillment / Ops

- **Fulfillment:** launch **pre-order/made-to-order + self-fulfillment** (in-house is right below
  ~400–500 orders/mo, and hand-inspection/hand-packing *is* the premium touch). Made-to-order
  flips the cash cycle (customer cash before production; needs only sampling capital ~$2–10k vs
  $20–100k for a full run). Move to an apparel-experienced 3PL near 400–500 orders/mo.
- **Shipping (Shopify):** **free domestic over a threshold (~1.3–1.5× AOV), flat below**;
  **carrier-calculated live rates international**; insure + require tracking/signature on a $300+
  jacket. Re-check flat rates against 2025/26 carrier increases.
- **Cross-border duties:** US $800 de-minimis removed (Aug 29 2025) — use **DDP** (seller pays
  duties, no doorstep surprise) via **Shopify Markets**; keep correct HS codes; IOSS for the EU.
  Enable DDP for priority countries first (US/UK/EU/CA/AU).
- **Returns:** **attack sizing pre-purchase** (rigorous size guide, fit indicators, try-on video —
  fit causes ~50% of returns). **Exchange-first, not refund-first.** 14–30 day window,
  "unworn/tags" conditions, inspect on receipt. Free domestic exchanges/returns; customer-paid
  international.
- **Packaging/unboxing** = high-leverage, low-cost UGC driver (premium unboxing → 40–60% more
  UGC). Design the box to be filmed: rigid mailer, dust bag, care card, numbered authenticity card,
  founder's note. Recyclable/minimal packaging *is* the Swiss aesthetic.
- **Delivery expectations:** reliability > speed (35% abandon a retailer after one late delivery).
  For made-to-order, **sell the wait as a feature** and narrate it in the post-purchase flow.
- **Inventory/cash:** let demand fund inventory (pre-order); **order shallow, reorder fast**
  (~300–500 units protects liquidity); target 6–12× turnover; concentrate SKUs.

# First 90 days (in order)
**1–30:** lock positioning/price/founder story · shoot brand film + short-form bank · stand up
Shopify + Klaviyo + **waitlist page** · daily organic posting · finalize supplier terms (small
run or full pre-order).
**31–60:** micro-creator seeding (15–30) · build the 3 Klaviyo flows · ops setup (fulfillment,
premium unboxing, size guide, exchange-first returns, reviews app) · configure shipping + DDP.
**61–90:** 7–10 day countdown → **limited first drop/pre-order to the waitlist first** · turn on
small retargeting-weighted paid (~$50–150/day) behind proven creative + live reviews · measure &
gate weekly (CAC, AOV, contribution margin, return rate, email %) · plan reorder + first line
extension.
