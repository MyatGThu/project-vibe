    What these numbers cover

    • Source: ABS Australian National Accounts – State Final Demand (dataflow ANA_SFD), current prices, original series, quarterly. Population: ABS Estimated Resident Population (ERP_Q).
    • Included: government final consumption (wages of public servants, goods and services bought to run schools, hospitals, police, defence etc.) and public investment (roads, buildings, equipment) by general government and public corporations, located in each state.
    • Commonwealth consumption is federal spending that takes place in that state. State & local covers the state government and its councils.
    • Not included: transfer payments such as pensions, JobSeeker, family payments, subsidies and interest. Those appear in Government Finance Statistics, which the ABS does not yet publish through its API by state.
    • Taxes: ABS Taxation Revenue, Australia, 2024-25 (5506.0). State & local taxes are those the state government and its councils levy themselves. GST is a Commonwealth tax and is not included, even though states receive it.
    • Debt: ABS Government Finance Statistics, Annual, 2024-25 (5512.0), balance sheets of each state's general government and non-financial public sectors.
    • Company tax: ATO Corporate Tax Transparency, Report of Entity Tax Information 2024-25 (data.gov.au).
    • Federal contracts: AusTender contract notices for 2025-26 from the free AusTender OCDS API. A July–September 2026 run with the Apify actor knotty_mistveil/austender-contract-notices gave the same figures.
    • A financial year runs July to June. "Per person" divides the year's spending by the state's population in the June quarter (or the latest quarter published).
