The code.

A five-person house making one thing well — a leather jacket built like armor.

A jacket should protect you and say nothing about it. That is the whole idea: the leather jacket as the modern man's armor — quiet strength, cut clean, worn as a code.

We make one, in full-grain hide — the top layer left on, the strongest, the one that earns a patina instead of cracking. Box-stitched where it's stressed, lined to move, finished by hand. No season, no logo, no noise. You buy it once.

Everything is made to order and numbered. We buy no dead stock, cut no corners you can't see, and answer to the person wearing it — not a calendar.
